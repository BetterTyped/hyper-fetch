# App dashboard

The **App** page is the first screen you see when you open a connected app. Its three tabs answer the first questions
of any debugging session: are requests failing, which endpoints are slow, and is the cache doing its job.

| Tab             | Answers                                                                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **General**     | How many requests ran, how many succeeded, what ran last and what fails most    |
| **Performance** | How fast responses are, which endpoint is slowest and how often requests fail   |
| **Cache**       | How much of the traffic is cached, how big the cache is and what is not cached  |

The header shows the app's name, its base URL and its environment badge. All numbers update live and cover everything
HyperFlow received since the app connected.

---

## General

<Screenshot
  src="/img/hyper-flow/app-general.webp"
  alt="General tab of the Acme Store app with the Network Overview card on the left and the Recent Requests table on the right."
/>

- **Network Overview** shows the adapter, the total and successful requests, the data received and sent, the average
  response and payload sizes, and the success rate.
- **Recent Requests** lists the last 8 requests with their status, endpoint, cache state, time and response time.
  **View All Requests** opens the [Network](/docs/hyper-flow/features/network.mdx) page.
- **Top traffic by Endpoint** ranks the 10 endpoints that received the most requests, with their share of all traffic.
- **Error Summary** lists the 5 endpoints and status codes that failed most often.

---

## Performance

<Screenshot
  src="/img/hyper-flow/app-performance.webp"
  alt="Performance tab with request patterns, response time percentiles, a chart of average response time by method and the slowest endpoints table."
/>

- **Request Patterns** names the most used request, the slowest request with its average time, and the error rate.
- **Response Times** shows the average, p50, p95 and p99 response times over the last 1,000 requests, the average
  response size and a chart of the average response time per HTTP method.
- **Slowest Endpoints** ranks 10 endpoints by average response time, next to their average processing time and
  response size.

:::tip Find a slow endpoint

Sort out a slow screen in two steps: check **Slowest Endpoints** for the endpoint the screen calls, then open it on the
[Network](/docs/hyper-flow/features/network.mdx) page and compare the timings of single requests.

:::

## Cache

<Screenshot
  src="/img/hyper-flow/app-cache.webp"
  alt="Cache tab with cache usage, cache size and entry count, a treemap of the cache distribution, and tables of the most cached endpoints and requests that are not cached."
/>

- **Cache Metrics** shows the share of requests stored in the cache, the cache size, the number of entries and a map of
  which entries take the most space.
- **Most Cached Endpoints** lists the endpoints with the most entries and their size. Many entries for one endpoint
  usually mean many param or query param combinations.
- **Not Cached Requests** lists requests sent with the cache turned off.

For a list of every entry with its freshness and observers, open the [Cache](/docs/hyper-flow/features/cache.mdx) page.
