# DB Inspector

The DB Inspector shows what a [`@hyper-fetch/db`](/docs/db/overview) db holds: its collections, the subsets they
loaded, the live queries with their plans, the pending optimistic writes and the outbox.

:::tip Purpose

1.  **See the plan**: every live query shows which loader serves it, what it sends and what runs on the client.
2.  **See freshness**: each loaded subset shows whether it is fresh, live through syncs, loading or failed.
3.  **See pending writes**: optimistic writes and the offline outbox are listed until they settle.

:::

---

## Inspecting your db

Pass the db to the devtools plugin of your client:

```tsx
import { DevtoolsPlugin } from "@hyper-fetch/plugin-devtools";

client.addPlugin(
  DevtoolsPlugin({
    appName: "My App",
    // highlight-next-line
    dbs: [db],
  }),
);
```

The plugin sends a snapshot whenever the db changes, at most once per tick, and one whenever HyperFlow connects.

---

## What it shows

- **Collections** with their row count, loaded subsets and pending writes. Select a collection to list its subsets:
  the loader that serves each, its rows, its state and how many queries read it.
- **Live queries** with their status, row count, plan, the conditions sent and the ones run locally, the syncs that
  keep them live, and their subscribers.
- **Outbox** entries of an offline db, in replay order.

Several dbs show as buttons above the collections.

:::secondary Summary

- Add `dbs: [db]` to the devtools plugin to inspect a db.
- The DB page shows collections, subsets, live queries with their plans and the outbox.

:::
